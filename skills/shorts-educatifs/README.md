# shorts-educatifs

Makes an episode of Roogo's Rasmané series: a 9:16 paper-cut educational comedy short (40 to 68 s) that ends on one lesson and a WhatsApp call to action.

- **Use it for:** the next episode of the series, a diaspora variant, or an agent (for example in HyperFrames) that must produce one.
- **Needs:** the Roogo knowledge vault (series bible, `series.json`, checklist, hashtag rules), a toolkit `studio.py` with `doctor`, `images`, `voix`, `build`, `mesure`, and keys for image and voice providers kept in the system keychain (never in files).
- **Flow:** sourced facts, voice-first script approved by the owner, one image per scene, voices, edit, loudness check, social captions, vault record.
- **Limits:** no fee or price claims, no promise of results, scripts are drafts until approved, sound is checked by ear.
- **Deliverables:** the mp4, the script and `episode.json`, the social captions file, and 1 to 3 improvement candidates for the skill.
