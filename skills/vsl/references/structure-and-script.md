# Structure and script

## Structure that worked (about 80 seconds)

| Part | Seconds | Shown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Promise | 6 | Four real exteriors, fast cuts |
| Pain 1: the empty house | 9 | Five real vacant or unfinished properties |
| Pain 2: calls go nowhere | 6 | Generated phone clip |
| Pain 3: chasing the rent | 8 | The same owner: 4 s riding a motorbike in profile, 4 s knocking at a tenant's gate with a notebook |
| Mechanism | 12 | Real interiors cycling beside four step cards |
| Price objection | 20 | Three cards that appear on the spoken words |
| Alternative | 5 | Three pack cards |
| Ask | 12 | Outro: store badges, website, WhatsApp number |

Every beat has voice over it. Cards appear when their words are spoken, not before.

## Script rules

- Simple spoken French. Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- Open on the payoff, not on an introduction. Name the pain the way the viewer would.
- Emotion comes from punctuation: an exclamation opener, a setup and reveal, a
  question answered at once. No emotion tags.
- State fees plainly, in words, and say what happens if the service fails to
  deliver ("no tenant found, no placement fee"). Describe a recurring fee as
  recurring, never as a one-time fee.
- Do not promise speed, guaranteed results, or traction numbers.

## Voice

Narrator: Sandrine on Cartesia (`scripts/build_vo.py`). Respell in the spoken
text only; captions and on-screen text keep the normal spelling:

| On screen | Spoken text |
| --- | --- |
| Roogo | Rohgo |
| Burkina | Bourkina |
| Ouaga, Ouagadougou | Waga |
| Amounts and percentages | In words ("cinquante pour cent") |
| A phone number | French pairs ("soixante-sept, zéro zéro, soixante et un, seize") |

Transcribe the narration back and read the numbers and names. Speech-to-text
will write "Rogo": replace it with "Roogo" on screen.

## Social caption (French)

Hook line, two or three lines of benefit, the fee wording if it is quoted, the
website on its own line, "Appel ou WhatsApp" with the number, the stores line,
about ten hashtags. No em dashes.
