# Static poster, on request only

The poster is not part of the default bundle. Build it only when the requester
asks for a static image.

## Canvas

1080x1350, a 4:5 ratio, sized for feed placement. This ratio belongs to the
poster alone; the video is always 1080x1920.

## Layout, top to bottom

On a solid terracotta field:

1. the logo on a white rounded chip;
2. a giant audience-qualifier headline, the first word in white near 150
   pixels and the second in navy near 96 pixels, for example the property type
   above the word "wanted";
3. the location and criteria as short cream sublines;
4. the photograph in a white-framed rounded card, with a navy URGENT tag when
   the requester stated urgency;
5. a navy budget pill;
6. one call-to-action question line;
7. the phone number as the largest element on the frame, 96 pixels or more.

The audience-qualifier word must be headline-sized. That word is what makes the
right owner stop scrolling.

## What not to do

- Do not default to a white background with gray panels. That layout exists
  only because one brief demanded it and it reads as off-brand.
- Do not let an image model render any text.
- Do not scatter small orange details across the frame instead of committing to
  the flat brand-color field.
