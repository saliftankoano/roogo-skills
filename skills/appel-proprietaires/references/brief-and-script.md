# Demand brief and script

## Extract the brief before anything else

Push back and ask when a field is missing. One ad states one demand.

| Field | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Property type | shop, house, commercial space |
| Zone and landmarks | the road to a named district, between two well-known buildings |
| Intended use | selling cosmetics, family housing |
| Budget | a monthly rent in FCFA |
| Urgency | "it is urgent" adds an URGENT tag and "this week" framing |
| Contact number | the campaign number to display and to speak |

Confirm the spelling of every place name with the requester. Burkinabe
neighborhood names are frequently misspelled by transcription and by memory,
and a misspelled zone destroys the ad's credibility with the exact owners it
targets.

## Script formula

Roughly thirty seconds of speech, sequenced so the owner hears the reason to
act before the instructions:

1. **Loss-framing hook** naming the owner's loss, for example an empty shop on
   a named road costing a stated amount every month.
2. **Who is paying**, a real client searching right now.
3. **Where**, the zone and its landmarks.
4. **For what**, the intended use.
5. **Budget**, stated plainly.
6. **Why now**, the urgency if the requester stated one.
7. **What the owner gains**, including how the rent reaches them.
8. **Call to action** with the phone number spoken in words.

Get the script approved before generating narration, images, or music. The
script is cheap to change; a generated carousel is not.

## Claim discipline

- Do not promise a guarantee of rental. State that the rent is paid to the
  owner through the platform.
- Do not invent client details, timelines, or amounts that the requester did
  not state.
- Do not advertise a property as available. This format calls owners in; it
  does not list anything.
