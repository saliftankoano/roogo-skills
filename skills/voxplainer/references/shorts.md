# Vertical social shorts

Use this mode for YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Reels, and similar 9:16 feeds.

## Defaults

When the user gives no delivery spec, use 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264 MP4 with AAC audio, and a duration appropriate to the idea—usually 20–60 seconds. Treat platform limits as changeable and verify them when the exact current limit matters.

## Story shape

- Earn attention in the opening second with the tension, surprising fact, or consequential question—not a greeting or logo animation.
- Establish context only after the viewer understands why the topic matters.
- Advance one claim at a time using short visual beats and deliberate pattern changes.
- Resolve the opening tension and finish on a memorable takeaway or requested call to action.

Draft to the target spoken duration; do not rely on speeding up a long script. Remove throat-clearing, repeated setup, and narration that merely describes what is already visible.

## Vertical composition

- Compose natively in 9:16. Favor a dominant central subject and a clear top-to-bottom hierarchy.
- Reserve room for burned-in captions and common platform interface overlays. Keep critical text and labels away from extreme edges and lower controls.
- Use large type, short lines, and few simultaneous elements. Check legibility at phone size.
- Reframe maps, charts, and comparisons for the narrow canvas rather than shrinking a horizontal graphic.
- Change the visual state frequently enough to sustain attention, but preserve brief holds for facts that must be read.

## Captions and audio

Use word- or phrase-timed captions when timing data is available. Break captions by meaning, emphasize only key words, and avoid covering the visual evidence. Keep music and sound effects subordinate to narration and verify intelligibility on phone speakers.

## Review frames

Inspect at minimum the hook frame, first caption-heavy frame, densest information frame, major visual transition, and final frame. Review the whole edit once with sound and once muted; the argument should remain followable in both conditions.
