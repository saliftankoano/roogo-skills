# Horizontal YouTube long-form

Use this mode for 16:9 editorial explainers with room for evidence, context, and chaptered reasoning.

## Defaults

When the user gives no delivery spec, use 1920x1080, 30 fps, H.264 MP4 with AAC audio. Let the research and argument determine duration; do not stretch a thin idea to an arbitrary target.

## Narrative architecture

Open with the central tension and a concrete promise. Build a causal argument rather than a list of facts. A useful structure is:

1. cold open or revealing moment;
2. concise thesis and stakes;
3. essential background;
4. escalating evidence or competing explanations;
5. synthesis, implication, and final takeaway.

Use chapters when they help the viewer understand a genuine shift in question, time, place, scale, or evidence. Reorient the viewer after complex passages and pay off questions introduced earlier.

## Horizontal visual system

- Use the canvas for comparisons, maps, timelines, and relationships; avoid filling every area simply because space exists.
- Maintain continuity of layout, geography, scale, and visual metaphors across scenes.
- Mix visual modes—footage, photos, documents, maps, charts, illustration, and kinetic type—according to the evidence.
- Introduce purposeful pattern changes at natural argument beats. Avoid visual churn that competes with comprehension.
- Give charts and documents enough hold time to be understood. Direct attention with highlights, masks, crops, arrows, and camera movement.

## Evidence and sourcing

Maintain a source ledger connecting claims and assets to their origins. Include on-screen source labels when the user or subject requires them. Preserve important caveats in the narration or visual treatment; do not turn uncertainty into certainty for dramatic effect.

## Edit and review

Review chapter transitions, cumulative pacing, visual repetition, voice consistency, music continuity, and whether later scenes depend on facts the video never established. Check representative frames from every chapter, then watch the complete encoded preview without stopping before final delivery.

When adapting the project into a short, rewrite around one self-contained insight and recomposite the visuals for 9:16. Do not summarize every chapter or crop the long-form master.
