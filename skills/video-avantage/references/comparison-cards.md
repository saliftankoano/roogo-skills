# Comparison and explainer cards

When a benefit needs a concrete before-and-after or an us-versus-them
comparison, build it as a phone-mockup card, which matches the established
static-ad style.

## Anatomy

A white rounded card inside a dark phone bezel, containing:

1. a kicker line above the title;
2. the title;
3. itemised rows, label on the left and value on the right, with costs and red
   flags in red and gains in green;
4. a divider;
5. a bold total.

Keep row labels short. A label plus a six or seven digit currency figure is
already tight on a card around 780 pixels wide.

## Numbers discipline

Reuse the approved reference model for a claim. Do not re-derive a different
set of numbers for a new creative; the same comparison has already been
reconciled across the static ads and earlier videos, and two versions of the
same claim in market is worse than a weaker single version.

Bring the exact figures to the requester for approval before they are rendered,
and never present an illustrative example as an actual client outcome.

## Placement

A card is a beat, not a backdrop. It appears on the line that explains it, and
the captions for that segment are positioned clear of it; see
[captions.md](captions.md).
