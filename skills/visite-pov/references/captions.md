# French captions for posting

Staff paste the caption with the video in WhatsApp, then post it. These
copy-paste captions may use a light touch of emoji and real blank lines. No em
dashes.

## Structure

1. A headline line in capitals: type and place, or the audience question for a
   targeted variant.
2. One or two sentences of description from the listing.
3. Three fact lines (location, use, documents), then the price line.
4. "Avec Roogo, on s'occupe de tout, de la visite jusqu'au notaire."
5. "📞 Appel ou WhatsApp : +226 67 00 61 16", then "🌐 roogobf.com" on its own
   line, then "Toutes nos annonces sur roogobf.com et sur l'application Roogo."
6. About ten hashtags: #Roogo, the neighborhood, #Ouagadougou #Ouaga
   #BurkinaFaso, the property type (#ParcelleAVendre #TerrainAVendre),
   #ImmobilierBurkina; add #DiasporaBurkinabe #InvestirAuBurkina for diaspora
   variants.

Add the direct listing link only when its address matches the property. Do not
promise things the listing does not state, for example that a buyer abroad can
sign without being present.
